## DM Template

### Short version (X/Twitter DM):

```
Hey! I built GameLaunch (gamelaunch-nine.vercel.app) - a platform specifically for AI-made browser games. Players can play your game directly on the site, vote, leave comments, and tip you via Stripe.

Would you want [GAME NAME] listed? Takes 2 minutes to submit. You keep 100% of tips.

No pressure either way, just think your game deserves more visibility.
```

### Longer version (for bigger devs):

```
Hey [NAME] - congrats on [GAME NAME], it's genuinely impressive.

I'm building GameLaunch, a platform for AI-made browser games. Think itch.io but specifically for vibe-coded/AI-assisted games, with:
- Your game embedded directly (iframe)
- Real voting from players
- Comments/feedback from the community  
- Stripe tipping (100% to you, we take nothing right now)

We're early but growing the catalog with quality games. Would love to have yours on there. Takes 2 min to submit at gamelaunch-nine.vercel.app/submit.

No exclusivity, no cost. Just more eyeballs on your work.
```
